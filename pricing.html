FAQ

What is a “map session”?

A session begins when you upload a map in the app and ends when you refresh or leave the page. This keeps pricing fair: you can fix mistakes and re-download as many times as needed without paying again.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use the app without signing in. Accounts may be added later to allow for saved projects.

Why include CSV if GeoJSON is the spatial format?

GeoJSON is best for GIS tools. CSV is useful for quick QA, spreadsheets, and lightweight workflows (and many GIS tools can import CSV as points).

Can I refine GCPs and re-export?

Yes — that’s the idea. A session lets you refine georeferencing and point placement, then re-download without extra cost.

Will you support SHP downloads?

SHP downloads may be developed in the future. Please let us know if you are interested.
