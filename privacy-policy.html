Privacy Policy – MyMapData

Last updated: February 7, 2026

MyMapData ("we", "our", or "us") provides a web-based tool for georeferencing maps and exporting point data.

Information We Collect

  • Uploaded maps and point data: Processed entirely in your browser. We do not store uploaded map files or extracted point data on our servers.
  • Payment information: Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We do not store or have access to your full credit card details.
  • Email address: Collected by Stripe during checkout for receipts and customer support.
  • Session identifiers: A temporary browser-based identifier is used to manage access after payment.

How We Use Information

  • To process payments and provide access to paid features
  • To respond to support requests
  • To maintain and improve the service

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Stripe for payment processing (https://stripe.com/privacy)
  • Cloudflare for hosting and security

Data Storage and Security

  • Map files and extracted data are processed client-side and are not persisted on our servers.
  • Limited transaction metadata (payment status, session identifier) is stored securely for access control.
  • We use industry-standard security practices provided by Stripe and Cloudflare.

Data Retention

  • Payment records are retained as required for accounting and legal purposes.
  • Temporary session identifiers expire automatically.

Your Rights

You may contact us to request information about or deletion of your personal data, where applicable.
